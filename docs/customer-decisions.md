# Customer decisions before real CVs

These do not block synthetic development. They must be resolved before real-data activation.

- Approve actual hiring rubrics, essential definitions and acceptable equivalent evidence for at least three representative roles. Decide who can publish criteria, make essential exceptions and finalise.
- Decide whether UK-exclusive processing is mandatory. Direct Claude API is not treated as a UK-exclusive commitment. If required, approve a verified regional route with a successful account test and written regional confirmation. No silent overseas fallback.
- Approve purpose, lawful basis, candidate notice, DPIA assessment, processor contracts, subprocessors, transfers and retention for files, text, model outputs, exports, logs and backups. Obtain qualified advice for actual employment/privacy arrangements.
- Name administrator, reviewers, support and incident owner; provide approved authentication sender/domain and account invitations. Confirm MFA and access-removal policy.
- Agree evaluation thresholds from specification section 10 before tuning: 45 synthetic CVs across three roles, 30 held out, independent recruiter labels, second reviewer checks, essential-case review, repeated runs and 20 adversarial pairs.
- Approve supervised pilot scope, reviewer training, correction route and three timed 20-CV comparisons. Accuracy/fairness/time benefits have not been measured.
- Agree code ownership, dependency licences, ongoing updates, support, budget, restore ownership and customer acceptance exercise.

No customer samples, approved rubrics, privacy decisions or independent labels are assumed to exist. All built-in records are fictional.
