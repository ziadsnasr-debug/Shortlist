# Phase 4: role templates

Shortlist includes three versioned, built-in role presets: customer success
manager, accounts assistant and service desk analyst. They are editable starting
points, not quality labels or automated recommendations. Each preset contains a
complete rubric with at most 12 criteria and exactly 100 points.

Templates are available only to administrators while a batch is unpublished
and its rubric is empty. Loading a template creates fresh criterion IDs and
marks the draft unsaved. Existing draft criteria are never replaced by a
template picker; the existing editable example action remains available as an
explicit replacement action with undo. Saving uses the existing rubric action,
workspace version and validation rules. Published or finalised batches never
load or mutate templates.

The catalogue is code-owned and immutable. A user-saved template library is
deferred until a future specification adds its ownership, versioning and
retention rules.
